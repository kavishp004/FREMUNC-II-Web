About FREMUNC

A one-day Model United Nations conference built for delegates at every level of experience.

The conference

What FREMUNC is

FREMUNC is the annual Model United Nations conference hosted by Irvington High School in Fremont, California, now in its fourth year. Over a single day, student delegates from across the region step into the roles of diplomats — researching global issues, representing nations other than their own, and debating their way toward workable solutions.

Delegates are assigned a country and a committee, then spend the day in simulated UN sessions: opening speeches, moderated caucuses, informal negotiation blocs, and finally the drafting and voting of resolutions. It is equal parts research, public speaking, and thinking on your feet.

The conference is built to be welcoming to first-time delegates while still challenging experienced ones. Our staff are on hand throughout the day to help newer delegates find their footing, and committees range from approachable general assemblies to faster-moving simulations. FREMUNC is open to high school delegates.

Why take part

What delegates will gain

Public Speaking Confidence

Delegates speak on the floor, defend their positions, and think on their feet — in a room where everyone else is learning to do exactly the same thing.

Research & Critical Thinking

Preparing for committee means digging into real policy, weighing competing sources, and understanding an issue from a perspective that isn't your own.

Diplomacy & Negotiation

Resolutions never pass alone. Delegates learn to build blocs, trade concessions, and find middle ground between very different national interests.

Global Awareness

Topics are drawn from genuine international debates, so delegates leave with a sharper sense of how the world actually negotiates — and why it stalls.

Writing & Resolution Drafting

From position papers to working papers to final draft resolutions, delegates practice writing that has to be precise, persuasive, and procedurally correct.

Networking and New Friendships

A committee room full of delegates from other schools is one of the easiest places to meet people who care about the same things you do.
