Position Paper Guide

How to research, structure, format, and submit your position paper before the conference.

Open the full rubric

Opens the FREMUNC 2026 Position Paper Rubric, which also includes a sample paper you can use as a template.

Overview

What a position paper is

A position paper is an essay explaining your country’s or character’s position on the topic at hand, and the solutions you intend to pursue in committee.

Length
4 pages maximum, not including works cited.
Format
MLA format, Times New Roman 12 pt, double spaced.
Header
Left-aligned, including your name, character assignment, conference, and committee.
Deadlines
For research awards September 28 For award consideration October 3
Submission
By email to your committee chairs.

No AI-generated content

Delegates may not use or paraphrase any content generated by artificial intelligence. If your position paper is determined to have been written by AI, you will be immediately disqualified from any awards.

Structure

The four sections

Every position paper is made up of these four parts, in this order.

Past and Current International Action ~1 paragraph

Historical context and background on the topic at hand. This section does not include your delegation’s policy or any previous action your delegation has taken, unless it is globally relevant.

Country / Character Policy ~2 paragraphs

Your country’s or character’s stance and policies on the topic. This section covers only action your country has already taken — no proposed solutions.

Proposed Solutions 3–6 paragraphs

The main part of your paper. Lay out the solutions you intend to pursue in committee. They should be realistic and consistent with your character policy.

Response to Guiding Questions ~2 paragraphs

Respond to 2 of the guiding questions provided in your committee’s background guide.
