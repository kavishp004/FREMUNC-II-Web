Good to know

Before you come

Dress Code
Business attire is encouraged for all delegates. Appropriate attire includes:
  • Dress shirts, blouses, sweaters, or polos
  • Slacks, khakis, dress pants, skirts, or dresses
  • Blazers, ties, and suits are encouraged but not required
  • Dress shoes, flats, loafers, or clean sneakers
Please avoid overly casual clothing, including athletic wear, pajamas, or clothing with inappropriate graphics or language.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I need previous Model UN experience?

Not at all. FREMUNC is designed to welcome first-time delegates, and staff are on hand throughout the day to help you find your footing. Experienced delegates will find plenty to challenge them, too.

Who can attend?

FREMUNC is open to high school delegates.

How do I register, and is there a fee?

Use the Register button at the top of this page — the form goes live on August 11. Early registration (Aug 11–29) is $22 per delegate, or $20 for Irvington students. General registration (Aug 30–Sep 19) is $25. Registration closes September 19.

What should I bring on the day?

Bring your position paper (printed or on a device), something to write with and on, and your student ID. A full list is TBD.

Will there be awards?

Yes. Awards are recognized at the Closing Ceremony at the end of the day.

Who do I contact with questions?

Reach the FREMUNC team at TBD, or follow us on Instagram for the latest updates.
